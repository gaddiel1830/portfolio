## Pratt truss bridge

`Design and Testing of a 3D Printed Truss Bridge Structure in Three Report.docx`, dated May 8, 2025, by Jesus Gaddiel Fragoso Vallejo with help from Ailini Elorduy and Stephanie Gonzalez.

- Source for geometry, PLA material, 100% infill, SolidWorks model and simulation, methods of joints and sections, and three-point bending.
- Target: 250 lbf. Failure load is reported as approximately 756 N or 169 lb. These are the report's rounded figures; an exact conversion of 756 N is approximately 170 lbf. The website labels the numbers as reported and does not claim the target was achieved.
- Simulation agreed with the observed failure location, not necessarily a quantitatively validated strength prediction.
- The comparison box beam was modeled but not physically tested because of time constraints.
- Reported possible causes of failure are not treated as conclusively established.
